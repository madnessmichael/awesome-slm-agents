# Scope and inclusion policy

Focus: language-model agents with a dense backbone up to **10B parameters**, including multimodal models. This is an editorial convention, not a community standard. Record total and active parameters separately for MoE; small active size does not imply small storage. Larger comparators and mixed-size studies may be included when explicitly labeled.

- **Core:** directly studies small-model action, tools, training, memory, planning, or deployment.
- **Supporting:** directly informs SLM-agent design or measures a relevant trade-off; explain the connection.
- **Background:** foundations and perspectives needed to interpret the field.

A small model is not a small-compute experiment. Inspect hardware, precision, context, concurrency, tool services, retries, teachers, and training separately. A small GPU criterion is not equivalent to a parameter-count criterion.

Exclude generic compression and generic agent papers without a clear SLM-agent connection, marketing-only claims, inaccessible primary evidence, duplicate versions, and leaderboard fragments without conditions. No citation or star threshold.

AI-assisted entries may be published with explicit review provenance. They are not represented as human-curated or reproduced. Full-text and human-review flags must reflect work actually completed. This repository does not claim endorsement or inclusion by the central Awesome list.

Publication status needs a source. An author comment reporting acceptance is labeled author-reported until independently checked. Preprint means no stronger status is verified here, not necessarily that no later venue exists. A null field means not established in this review, not absent in the paper.
